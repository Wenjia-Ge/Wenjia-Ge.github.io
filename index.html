Publication

ICONE30 · Kyoto, Japan · 2023

Application of the Method of Manufactured Solutions to Verify a Nuclear Reactor Physics Computational Code using the Method of Characteristics

First-author conference paper on numerical verification for reactor physics computation. The work studies the order of accuracy of flat-source and linear-source approximations in MoC.

Wenjia Ge, et al. Application of the Method of Manufactured Solutions to Verify a Nuclear Reactor Physics Computational Code using the Method of Characteristics. ICONE30, Kyoto, Japan, 2023.
Using the Method of Manufactured Solutions, the study verifies that the flat-source approximation is second-order accurate and the linear-source approximation reaches fourth-order accuracy in planar geometry.
