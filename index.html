Patient-specific medical robotics simulation

Surgical concentric tube robot, advised by Prof. Rui Li

A simulation workflow for vascular modeling, path planning, and robot visualization in minimally invasive surgery research. It models the robot's curvature and motion inside constrained anatomy. I processed CTA-based anatomical models, extracted vessel centerlines with VMTK, and built the pipeline from a 3D Systems Touch haptic device through ROS2 into RViz and 3D Slicer. I handled hardware integration, real-time visualization, and validation. Next steps are tip tracking, coordinate alignment, and measuring progress along the vessel centerline.

Verifying the Method of Characteristics

First-author paper, ICONE30, Kyoto, Japan, 2023

I analyzed flat-source and linear-source approximations of the Method of Characteristics, a numerical method used in reactor physics, and verified them with the Method of Manufactured Solutions. I implemented the verification and measured error convergence across meshes and rays. In planar geometry, the flat-source approximation is second-order accurate and the linear-source approximation reaches fourth-order accuracy.

Wenjia Ge, et al. Application of the Method of Manufactured Solutions to Verify a Nuclear Reactor Physics Computational Code using the Method of Characteristics. ICONE30, Kyoto, Japan, 2023.

  • Reactor physics

IMU-based gesture recognition on STM32

Embedded systems project, STM32L475 Discovery board

A motion-driven gesture recognition system built around IMU sensing, gesture encoding, and onboard feedback. I implemented gyroscope calibration, three-step gesture recording, and dominant-axis matching, using the LSM6DSL IMU, UART monitoring, and LED feedback for interaction and debugging. The whole flow is written in C on STM32Cube and PlatformIO.
