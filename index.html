Medical robotics: surgical navigation and tactile sensing

Research Assistant, advised by Prof. Rui Li, Feb to Aug 2026

A simulation workflow for vascular modeling, path planning, and robot visualization in minimally invasive surgery research. It models the robot's curvature and motion inside constrained anatomy. I processed CTA-based anatomical models, extracted vessel centerlines with VMTK, and built the pipeline from a 3D Systems Touch haptic device through ROS2 into RViz and 3D Slicer. I handled hardware integration, real-time visualization, and validation, and resolved instability from WSL2 and USB passthrough.

On the sensing side, I set up and validated two tactile sensors with an Arduino and Python (pyserial) workflow that streams, plots, and analyzes both channels in real time. I characterized baseline noise and contact response, tuned detection thresholds from measured ADC ranges, and measured cross-talk between channels during simultaneous operation. I also explored waterproof encapsulation with PDMS, Ecoflex, and Parylene C, and defined bare-versus-coated metrics for sensitivity, baseline stability, noise, and response time.

Sb2S3 thin-film solar cells

Undergraduate thesis, Shenzhen University, Outstanding Thesis Award

I fabricated CdS and Sb2S3 thin films by chemical bath deposition and built planar FTO/CdS/Sb2S3/Spiro-OMeTAD/Au solar cells from them. I tested a thioacetamide surface treatment against untreated control devices and characterized both with XRD, SEM, contact-angle, J-V, and EQE measurements. The treatment reduced surface defects and porosity and raised power conversion efficiency from 4.43% to 5.43%. I operated the monochromator and spectrometer setup for the EQE measurements myself.

  • Thin films
  • Solar cells
  • XRD and SEM
  • J-V and EQE

Verifying the Method of Characteristics

First-author paper, ICONE30, Kyoto, Japan, 2023

I analyzed flat-source and linear-source approximations of the Method of Characteristics, a numerical method used in reactor physics, and verified them with the Method of Manufactured Solutions. I implemented the verification and measured error convergence across meshes and rays. In planar geometry, the flat-source approximation is second-order accurate and the linear-source approximation reaches fourth-order accuracy.

Wenjia Ge, et al. Application of the Method of Manufactured Solutions to Verify a Nuclear Reactor Physics Computational Code using the Method of Characteristics. ICONE30, Kyoto, Japan, 2023.

  • Numerical methods
  • Verification
  • Error analysis
  • Reactor physics

IMU-based gesture recognition on STM32

Individual embedded systems project, STM32L475 Discovery board

A motion-driven gesture recognition system built around IMU sensing, gesture encoding, and onboard feedback. I implemented gyroscope calibration, three-step gesture recording, and dominant-axis matching, using the LSM6DSL IMU, UART monitoring, and LED feedback for interaction and debugging. The whole flow is written in C on STM32Cube and PlatformIO.
