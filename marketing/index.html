Research interests

I am interested in consumer behavior on content platforms. How does a creator's point of view shape interest in a product? How do likes on short videos relate to purchase intent? How do professionals decide whether to adopt AI tools? I am also curious whether AI digital twins can capture individual preferences instead of averaging them away.

I come at these questions from two sides. I run content experiments with a combined audience of 160,000+ followers and have done audience research and outreach in industry. I also have an engineering and physics research background, so I am comfortable building data pipelines, running controlled comparisons, and analyzing the results.

Most relevant work

Audience behavior and content experiments

Self-directed, September 2024 to present

I run creator accounts on Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and other platforms, with 160,000+ combined followers. About 60,000 of them are on Douyin. I treat each post as a small experiment: I vary the theme, format, hook, posting time, and visual direction, read the engagement data, and keep what the audience responds to.

A new account I started grew from 0 to 10,000 followers in one month this way. I also collect audience feedback with questionnaires and analyze post performance in Excel. I have worked with brands on paid sponsored posts and KOC (key opinion consumer) collaborations.

  • Engagement data
  • Questionnaires
  • Brand collaborations

Health insurance information for international students

Content Marketing and Community Outreach, Kimber Health, New York, Jul to Sep 2026

I researched what international students at NYU, UCLA, and UChicago needed to know about health insurance, compared cost, eligibility, and deadlines for each school, and turned that into guides, FAQs, posts, and visuals. I hosted offline events and did in-person outreach in New York as a community ambassador, and logged prospective-customer inquiries in Salesforce. One RED post earned about 5,000 likes and saves.

Explaining robots to the people who will use them

Research Intern, Embodied AI and Robotics, AI² Robotics, Shenzhen, Oct 2025 to Jan 2026

I tested robot subsystems across the perception, decision, and action loop, then hosted three technical demos for external partners and wrote the scripts and materials myself. The demos had to connect what the robot could and could not do to each partner's own use case. Outcomes included partnerships with one company in Dubai and two in Singapore. I also did competitor benchmarking and product comparisons.
