Research interests

I am interested in human-robot and human–AI interaction. Questions I keep coming back to: how robots can show what they intend to do and read the people around them in shared spaces, how clinical teams work alongside robotic tools, whether AI creative tools make people's work more alike, and how errors in AI output change the way people judge what they read or hear.

My engineering work lets me build and test these systems, including a human-in-the-loop haptic pipeline and data tooling for human–AI dialogue studies. My audience work shows me how people actually respond: I run content experiments with a large audience and have explained robots to partners who would use them.

Most relevant work

Human-in-the-loop surgical navigation

Research Assistant, advised by Prof. Rui Li, Feb to Aug 2026

I built a real-time pipeline that lets a person steer through vascular anatomy by hand. A 3D Systems Touch haptic device sends input through ROS2 to RViz and 3D Slicer, where the motion is shown against anatomy built from CTA-based models and VMTK centerlines. The simulation models a concentric-tube surgical robot moving through constrained anatomy. I owned hardware integration, real-time visualization, and validation, and resolved instability from WSL2 and USB passthrough.

The project also included tactile sensing: I set up two tactile sensors with an Arduino and Python workflow, characterized noise and contact response, and measured cross-talk between channels.

Audience behavior and content experiments

Self-directed, September 2024 to present

I run creator accounts on Douyin, Xiaohongshu, and other platforms, with 160,000+ combined followers. About 60,000 of them are on Douyin. I treat each post as a small experiment: I vary the theme, format, hook, posting time, and visual direction, read the engagement data, and keep what the audience responds to.

A new account I started grew from 0 to 10,000 followers in one month this way. I also collect audience feedback with questionnaires and analyze post performance in Excel. I have worked with brands on paid sponsored posts and KOC (key opinion consumer) collaborations.

Visual spatial data for robot perception and training

NYU AI4CE Lab, advised by Prof. Chen Feng, 2026 to present

I collect visual and spatial data with MetaCam across indoor and outdoor scenes and process the raw output into point clouds and 3D scene representations. I inspect reconstructions in CloudCompare and MeshLab, flag artifacts, and build preprocessing and visualization workflows that raise dataset quality for robot training and world models. I also build HPC workflows for ingestion, 3D Gaussian Splatting reconstruction, and quality control of large-scale navigation data.

I am a student leader on the lab's Wanderland project, a vision-language navigation benchmark built on real-world 3D reconstructions and a CVPR 2026 Highlight. I collect data, track the team's collection progress, and onboard new members. Wanderland project page. Raw data is not public because of lab policy.
